### Stronger protection against traffic floods

Applications on `*.vertraweb.app` and on custom domains now have stronger protection against traffic floods: abusive visitors are blocked before they reach your application. Applications that receive a sudden traffic spike are no longer paused by Vertra Shield.

During a Shield pause, start, restart and configuration changes now show the reason and when the pause ends, instead of a generic access error (`APP_SHIELD_COOLDOWN`). See [Platform Protections](/knowledge-base#7-platform-protections-vertra-shield).
