### Applications page

Vertra Cloud got a public page for **applications** at `/apps`, in Portuguese, English and Spanish.

It explains the path from ZIP or GitHub to the container, the Bot and Website types, supported
runtimes, deploy methods and operational features: isolation, logs, metrics, snapshots,
environment variables, web publishing and custom domains.

It also documents the lifecycle and recovery after crashes, with FAQs published as structured data
for search engines.

### Database origins

The public PostgreSQL, MySQL, MongoDB and Redis pages now include a section on how each database
came to be, with historical milestones and a portrait of a central author or contributor.

The pages remain available in Portuguese, English and Spanish.

### New Enterprise experience

The Enterprise page now ends with a redesigned capacity configurator: memory, compute, project
limit and price change together as you pick a tier.

The SLA, premium support and the 4 GB to 32 GB scale are now clearer.
