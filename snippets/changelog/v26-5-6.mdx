### Upgrades charge only the difference

When you move to a bigger plan, you no longer pay the full price of the new plan on the spot.

- **More than 14 days left and the current period paid by Pix or card.** You pay only the difference between the price of the new plan and the price of your current plan, in your billing cycle and proportional to the days left. **Your expiration date does not change**, and the next renewal is already on the new plan, in the same cycle.
- **14 days or fewer left, or the current period not paid by Pix or card.** The upgrade is a purchase of the new plan for the period you choose, and the value of the days you have left becomes additional time on the new plan, as before. A period is not paid by Pix or card when it came from a redeemed code, a courtesy or a zero-value order.
- **Coupons and referral balance.** A valid coupon applies to the difference, with its usual rules. Referral balance can cover part of it.
- **Before you pay,** the checkout shows the difference, the days left, your expiration date and the price of the next renewal. You keep your billing cycle: the checkout does not ask for another period.

#### API

[Quote Order](/api-reference/endpoint/billing/quote-order) and [Create Order](/api-reference/endpoint/billing/create-order) return a `proration` object when an upgrade charges only the difference: the current plan, your billing cycle, the days left, the expiration date and the price of the next renewal. In that case the `months` you send is ignored. [List Orders](/api-reference/endpoint/billing/list-orders) and [Get Order Status](/api-reference/endpoint/billing/order-status) mark these orders with `prorated: true`.

### Downgrades keep your expiration date

A downgrade is still immediate and still moves one plan down, but it now keeps your expiration date.

- **Before:** the remaining days were converted into extra days on the smaller plan.
- **Now:** the expiration date stays as it is, and the difference for the remaining days is not converted into credit, additional time or a refund. The next renewal is on the smaller plan, at its price.
- **Open renewals.** An automatic renewal already created for the old plan is cancelled, and the next one is created on the smaller plan.
- **Clearer before you confirm.** The dashboard shows the new plan and your expiration date. The closer you are to your expiration date, the less of the bigger plan you leave unused.

The checks are the same: your memory usage and Docker images must fit the smaller plan, one downgrade every 3 hours, and the code sent to your email.
