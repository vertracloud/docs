Your Blob Storage quota depends on your plan and belongs to the account owner (workspace members share the owner's quota). The numbers live in the [knowledge base](/knowledge-base); this page is the Blob Storage view.

## Per plan

| Plan | Quota | Folder rules | Custom domains | Image transformations / month |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Economy | 1 GB | 5 | 0 | none |
| Pro | 5 GB | 5 | 0 | none |
| Scale | 15 GB | 5 | 0 | 600 |
| Intermediary | 20 GB | 5 | 0 | 1,000 |
| Enterprise-4 | 30 GB | 5 | 1 | 1,500 |
| Enterprise-6 | 50 GB | 5 | 1 | 2,000 |
| Enterprise-8 | 100 GB | 5 | 1 | 2,500 |
| Enterprise-12 and 14 | 150 GB | 10 | 3 | 4,000 |
| Enterprise-16 to 24 | 250 GB | 10 | 3 | 5,000 |
| Enterprise-26 to 32 | 250 GB | 20 | 10 | 10,000 |

Features that only some plans include:

| Feature | Available from |
|---|---|
| Upload rate of more than one per second | Enterprise-12 |
| Metadata (up to 5 keys, 512 bytes total) | Enterprise-12 |
| Password on [shares](/blob/sharing) | Enterprise-12 |
| `no-cache` as `cache_control` | Enterprise-26 |
| Expiration shorter than 7 days (minimum 1 hour) | Enterprise-26 |

Below Enterprise-12, uploads are limited to **one per second** per account. A gated feature answers `PLAN_RESTRICTED_FEATURE`.

Read your live values from `limits` in [Get stats](/api-reference/endpoint/blob/account/stats) instead of hardcoding them.

## Exceeding the quota

Uploads stop with `STORAGE_QUOTA_EXCEEDED` as soon as used space plus reserved space plus the new file would pass your quota. Nothing is deleted.

If you **downgrade** and your usage is above the new quota, your files stay. Uploads stay blocked until you are back under the quota, and the overage is billed at **R\$0.20 per started GB** on the next renewal. [Get stats](/api-reference/endpoint/blob/account/stats) shows `over_quota_bytes` and `estimated_overage_cents`. See [Plan downgrade](/plan-downgrade).

## Expiration and deletion

When your plan expires or is canceled, your files enter a **72-hour grace period**:

- Uploads and other writes are blocked and answer `BLOB_NAMESPACE_SUSPENDED`.
- Public files, temporary links and share pages keep working until the grace period ends.
- You receive e-mail reminders during the period.
- Renewing during the grace period restores everything immediately.
- After 72 hours the files are **permanently deleted, without a backup**.

A suspension for abuse is different: suspended files go offline right away.

<Warning>Deletion after the grace period cannot be undone. Download or move what you need before your plan expires.</Warning>
